GADGET HUB - APPLE WATCH IMAGE PACK (Series 6 and newer, 2020 to 2026)

FOLDERS
01_Product_photos
   Apple store lineup shots, 2000 px JPG. "case-unselect-gallery" = the watch cases; "band-unselect-gallery" = bands.
   Folders: Series 9 (2023), Series 10 (2024), Series 11 (2025), Series 12 (2026), SE, Ultra (2023 to 2026).
02_Material_colour_swatches_close-ups
   Small close-ups of each case finish (round or strip swatches). Use for colour dots in the UI. NOT product photos.
03_Animation_and_page_images_apple.com
   Hero, design, health, fitness, battery, safety, Hermes, Nike, kids pages. Use for scroll animations and "feel" sections.
   File names carry a random hash: build a manifest by scanning (use the part before the double underscore).
04_Compare_headers_and_swatches
   Apple compare headers for Series 12, SE 3, Ultra 4.
05_Colour_overviews_Apple_support
   One image per model showing the watch itself (front), from Apple's "identify your Apple Watch" page.
   Covers Series 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, SE (2020 to 2026), Ultra 1 to 4, and Hermes versions.

HONEST GAPS
- Apple does not publish one store photo per colour per model for Watch. For per-model main images use folder 05.
- Series 7 and 8 have no store gallery; use folder 05.
- No video files are included. Build animations from the frames in folder 03.
- Image names with "se" galleries mix SE generations 2022, 2023, 2025 and 2026; check each picture before assigning.
Apple owns the images. Fine for a design mock-up; live use needs permission.
