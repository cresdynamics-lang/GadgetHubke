GADGET HUB - AIRPODS IMAGE PACK (AirPods Pro 3, AirPods 5, AirPods Max, plus 2024 AirPods 4 and Pro 2)

FOLDERS
01_Product_photos
   Apple store shots, 2000 px JPG.
   AirPods_Pro_3: hero-select, gallery 1-5, hearing-aid/protection/test feature shots.
   AirPods_5_(newest_standard): hero-select, select (earbuds), wcc-select (wireless charging case), gallery 1-5.
   AirPods_Max_(USB-C_2024_and_newer): hero-select, one shot per colour (blue, midnight, orange, purple, starlight), gallery 1-5.
   AirPods_4_and_Pro_2_(2024): hero, select and gallery shots for the 2024 models.
02_Colour_swatches: tiny two-tone colour dots for AirPods Max (blue, midnight, orange). Use for colour selectors only.
03_Animation_and_page_images_apple.com: hero, design, sound, noise control, hearing, fitness, battery, case frames
   from the AirPods Pro, AirPods 5, AirPods Max and AirPods landing pages. File names carry a random hash;
   build a manifest by scanning (use the part before the double underscore).
04_Compare_and_feature_images: Apple compare images (noise control, adaptive audio, spatial audio, hearing aid, heart rate,
   live translation, lossless audio, case comparisons).
05_Case_and_model_overviews_Apple_support: charging case images from Apple's "identify your AirPods" page, transparent PNG.
   Covers cases for AirPods 1-3, Pro (1st, 2nd with Lightning or USB-C, 3rd), AirPods 4 and 5, wireless cases.

HONEST GAPS
- AirPods 2, AirPods 3, AirPods Pro (1st gen), AirPods Pro 2 (2022 and 2023) and the 2020 Lightning AirPods Max have no
  Apple store photo in this pack. Use the 2024 Pro 2 and AirPods 4 shots as stand-ins for the same-looking generation
  and the case overviews in folder 05 for older cases. Say so on the site.
- AirPods Max colour shots are the 2024 USB-C model. The 2020 colours (space gray, silver, sky blue, green, pink) are not included.
- No video files. Build animations from the frames in folder 03.
Apple owns the images. Fine for a design mock-up; live use needs permission.
