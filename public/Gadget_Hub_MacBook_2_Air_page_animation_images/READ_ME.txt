GADGET HUB - MacBook images, 2020 and newer (Air and Pro)
Source: Apple's own store, support and product pages. Images belong to Apple: fine for a design mock-up, permission needed for a live site.

01_Product_photos      Apple store product shots, 2000px wide, one per colour.
  MacBook_Air:  Air 2018-2020 chassis (M1, 2020, uses this same design; Apple's image is labelled 2018), 15in M2 2023, 13in M3 2024, 13in and 15in M4 2025, size comparison.
  MacBook_Pro:  13in M1 2020, 13in M2 2022, 14in/16in M1 Pro/Max 2021, M2 Pro/Max 2023, M3 family 2023, M4 family 2024 (plus the black/silver "hero" shots).
  Apple publishes ONE store shot per colour for Macs (not several angles like iPhone). Angles come from folder 03.
02_Colour_overviews_Apple_support   Apple's "identify your Mac" images: the open Mac plus every colour side by side. Covers Air 2020, 2022 (M2), 2023 (15in), 2024 (M3), 2025 (M4) and 2026 (M5), and Pro 13in 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, plus M5 Pro/Max.
03_Animation_and_page_images_apple.com   Images from Apple's current MacBook Air and MacBook Pro (M5) pages, sorted by section: hero, design (colours, sizes, gallery), display, camera, ports, battery, performance, Apple Intelligence, macOS, product-viewer, start and end frames used for scroll animations. Largest size available. Tiny icons removed.

NOT included: the page videos (streamed, very large, and Apple's). The start/end frames in folder 03 are what you animate between.
Gaps: no store shot for the 2022 M2 Air, the 2020 M1 Air (its own file), 15in M3 Air, or the M5 models. Use the folder 02 overviews and folder 03 images for those.
