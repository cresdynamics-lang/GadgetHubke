GADGET HUB - TV & HOME IMAGE PACK (Apple TV 4K, HomePod, HomePod mini)

FOLDERS
01_Product_photos (Apple store shots, 2000 px JPG)
   Apple_TV_4K: hero-select, gallery 1-5, "what's in the box" (box and Siri Remote).
   HomePod_2nd_gen: select, midnight, white, gallery 1-5.
   HomePod_mini: select (all colours), one shot per colour (blue, midnight, orange, white, yellow), gallery 1-5.
02_Colour_finish_swatches: close-up fabric finishes for HomePod (midnight, white) and HomePod mini (blue, midnight, orange, white, yellow).
03_Animation_and_page_images_apple.com: hero start/end frames, Siri Remote, performance, rooms, home control, FaceTime, intercom,
   sound frames from the Apple TV 4K, HomePod and HomePod mini pages, plus the TV & Home landing page.
   File names carry a random hash; build a manifest by scanning (use the part before the double underscore).
   Files starting with icon_ or logo_ are small glyphs for feature tiles.
04_Compare_images: Apple's HomePod vs HomePod mini compare images.
05_Model_overviews_Apple_support: Apple TV 4K 1st, 2nd and 3rd generation and the Apple TV HD, from Apple's "identify your Apple TV" page.

HONEST GAPS
- Apple does not publish separate store shots for the two Apple TV 4K versions (Wi-Fi and Wi-Fi + Ethernet); one set serves both. Use the 05 overviews for older generations.
- There is no image for the original HomePod (2018) and none for older HomePod mini colours beyond those listed.
- No video files are included. Build animations from the frames in folder 03.
Apple owns the images. Fine for a design mock-up; live use needs permission.
