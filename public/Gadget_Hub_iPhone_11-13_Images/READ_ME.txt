Gadget Hub - iPhone 11 to iPhone 16 image pack

Folders: iPhone_11, iPhone_12, iPhone_13, iPhone_14, iPhone_15, iPhone_16. Inside each, one folder per model, one set of files per colour.

File names: model_colour_viewN.jpg
  view1 = front and back of the phone together (large, about 1600 px wide)
  view2 = close-up of the rear camera corner (wide strip)
  view3 = close-up of the camera and screen corner (wide strip)
These are the three pictures Apple shows for each colour. They are not 360-degree spins.

All_colours_overview: one picture per model showing every colour from the back, from Apple's support pages. Use these for models where we have no angle set (see below).

Coverage notes:
- iPhone 11 Pro and 11 Pro Max: view1 and view3 only (Apple has no view2) in silver, gold, space grey and midnight green.
- iPhone 11 (standard), iPhone 12 Pro, iPhone 12 Pro Max, iPhone 16e: no angle set found, overview picture only.
- iPhone 13 Pro and 13 Pro Max: graphite, gold and silver only. Sierra Blue and Alpine Green are in the overview picture only.
- iPhone 13 and 13 mini include green and (PRODUCT)RED. iPhone 14 and 14 Plus include yellow.
- Titanium colours on the 15 Pro and 16 Pro families are named blacktitanium, whitetitanium, bluetitanium, naturaltitanium, deserttitanium.

Rights: these pictures belong to Apple. They are fine for mock-ups. Before they go on a live website the shop should have permission from Apple or its supplier.
