GADGET HUB - APPLE PENCIL IMAGE PACK (Accessories category)

FOLDERS
01_Store_gallery_and_feature_images
   Apple store gallery shots (pencil with iPad Pro / iPad Air, 2023 to 2025), "unselect" and "select" galleries for Pencil Pro,
   Pencil (USB-C) and Pencil (2nd generation), plus the 13 feature images: precision, latency, tilt, pressure, magnetic charging,
   charging, hover, double-tap, barrel roll, squeeze, haptic, Find My, engraving. Also the Pencil Pro and Pencil USB-C splitter images.
02_Product_photos_by_part_number
   Plain product shots on white, 2000 px. Apple_Pencil_Pro (MX2D3), Apple_Pencil_USB-C (MUWA3), Apple_Pencil_2nd_gen (MU8F2).
03_Model_overviews_Apple_support
   Landscape crops of the Pencil 1st gen, 2nd gen, Pencil Pro and the newer Apple Pencil (USB-C) from Apple's identify page. Use for compatibility charts and older models.
04_Animation_and_page_images_apple.com
   Frames from apple.com/apple-pencil (hero, features, apps and creative scenes). File names carry a random hash;
   build a manifest by scanning and use the part before the double underscore as the key. Prefer the _large_2x file.

HONEST GAPS
- No store product shot for Apple Pencil (1st generation) in this pack. Use the support overview in folder 03.
- Apple Pencil (USB-C) and Pencil Pro have only a few product angles each (3 images). Use the gallery and feature images for more.
- No video files. Build animations from the feature frames.
Apple owns the images. Fine for a design mock-up; live use needs permission.
