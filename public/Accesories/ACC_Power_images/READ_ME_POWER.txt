GADGET HUB - ACCESSORIES: POWER (Apple-made chargers, MagSafe and cables)
Source: apple.com store product pages. Folder names carry the product name; file names are Apple's part numbers.
01_Power_adapters: 20W, 35W dual, 40W dynamic, 70W, 96W, 140W adapters + extension cable.
02_MagSafe_and_wireless_charging: MagSafe Charger 1m / 2m, Apple Watch Magnetic Fast Charger to USB-C.
03_Charge_and_Thunderbolt_cables: 60W and 240W USB-C charge cables, USB-C to MagSafe 3, Thunderbolt 4 Pro (1.8m, 3m), Thunderbolt 5 Pro (1m).
NOTES FOR THE OWNER
- Apple store image names differ from the product code for some items (e.g. 70W adapter = MQLN3, 60W cable = MQKJ3, 240W cable = MU2G3, 96W adapter = MW2L3). Wattage, cable length and compatibility must be checked against Apple's pages before launch.
- Files ending _GEO_US show a US two-pin plug. Kenya uses the UK-style three-pin plug, so confirm which plug variant you stock and do not show a US plug as the Kenyan product.
- The extension cable has only one photo (MK122). No colour variants exist for these items.
- Third-party chargers (Belkin, Nimble, Beats cables etc.) are NOT included.
- Apple owns these images. Permission is needed for live use.
