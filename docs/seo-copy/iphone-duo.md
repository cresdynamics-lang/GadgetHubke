#### Design and display
The frame and hinge cover are Grade 5 titanium. The front is Ceramic Shield 2 and the back is Ceramic Shield.

Closed, you use the 5.4-inch Super Retina XDR outer display like a normal compact iPhone: one hand, quick tasks, pocket-friendly. Open, the 7.6-inch Super Retina XDR folding display gives you room to:
- run two apps side by side;
- read a full page without zooming;
- watch video on a far bigger screen than any other iPhone.

The inner display also has a nano-texture finish, which reduces reflections in bright light.

#### Performance
The iPhone Duo uses the A20 Pro, the same chip as the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max, and it is vapor-cooled for sustained Pro performance. On a folding phone that matters, because the big screen makes multitasking more likely. It keeps up with a video call on one side and notes on the other, or a spreadsheet next to your email. Apple Intelligence features run on the device too.

#### Camera
The iPhone Duo has a 48MP Dual Fusion camera system. Smart Take and advanced capture modes are designed to help you get a well-timed, well-exposed shot without fiddling with settings. Because the phone folds, you can stand it partly open on a table for hands-free photos, group shots and video calls.

#### Battery and charging
The Duo has a dual-battery system for all-day power. Apple rates video playback at up to 44 hours on the outer display and up to 31 hours on the inner display. If you mostly use the outer screen and open it for longer tasks, you get through a full working day comfortably. It charges over USB-C and supports MagSafe.

The box includes a USB-C charge cable but no power adapter.
