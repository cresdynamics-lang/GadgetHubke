The iPhone 18 Pro uses a forged aluminium unibody, with Ceramic Shield 2 on the front and Ceramic Shield on the back. The 6.3-inch Super Retina XDR display has ProMotion up to 120Hz, so scrolling and games feel fluid. The Dynamic Island can show up to three Live Activities at once.

#### Performance
The A20 Pro chip has a dual 16-core Neural Engine. A next-generation vapor chamber keeps it cool, which helps during long video edits, graphics-heavy games and Apple Intelligence tasks. In daily use, apps open quickly and the phone stays responsive when you switch between M-Pesa, WhatsApp, maps and the camera. It supports 5G on Kenyan networks that offer it.

#### Camera
There are three 48MP cameras on the back:

- **Main camera:** 48MP Fusion with a variable aperture from ƒ/1.48 to ƒ/4.0, at 24 mm and 48 mm (1x and 2x). You can open it up for low light, or close it down for sharper group shots and landscapes.

- **Telephoto:** 48MP at 100 mm and 200 mm (4x and 8x optical quality), so you can fill the frame from the back of a hall or the stands at a match.

On the front, the 18MP Center Stage camera keeps you centred on video calls and group selfies.

#### Battery and charging
Apple rates the iPhone 18 Pro at up to 36 hours of video playback. With a compatible USB-C adapter, wired charging reaches up to 50% in about 15 minutes, which is handy before you head out for the evening. MagSafe and Qi wireless charging are supported.
